# Brand Content Guide

## B1 Plain, specific tone
Write short factual sentences. Lead with what the item is and who it suits. Avoid hype and filler words.

## B2 Title pattern
Use Brand + Product + Key attribute, for example "Acme Trail Backpack 30L - Green".

## B3 Description structure
Start with a one-line summary, then key features, then condition and what is included in the sale.

## B4 No pressure language
Avoid urgency tactics such as "act now", "limited time" or "last chance" unless a real dated offer exists.
