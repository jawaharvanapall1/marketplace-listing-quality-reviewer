# Marketplace Policy

## P1 Required information
Every listing must include a title, description, category, price and seller. Listings missing core information are not published.

## P2 Accurate, readable titles
Titles must describe the item. No keyword stuffing, ALL CAPS, repeated symbols or promotional text. Titles are 10-80 characters.

## P3 Truthful claims
Superlatives and absolute claims (best, #1, guaranteed, 100%, cheapest, perfect) need verifiable evidence or must be removed.

## P4 Prohibited items
Counterfeit or replica goods, weapons, ammunition, tobacco and vaping products, and prescription drugs may not be listed.

## P5 Health and medical claims
Products may not claim to cure, treat or prevent disease, or imply regulatory approval (for example FDA approved) without documentation.

## P6 Price transparency
Price must be stated as a plain number. The description must not contradict it or hide fees such as "call for price" or "shipping extra".

## P7 Keep communication on-platform
Phone numbers, email addresses, WhatsApp handles or external payment links must not appear in listings.

## P8 Condition disclosure
Condition (new, used, refurbished) and known defects must be stated for physical goods.

## P9 Attribute consistency
Attributes (brand, size, colour, model, material) must agree with the title and description and must not be empty for the category.

## P10 Category accuracy
The listing must be placed in the supported category that best matches the item.

## P11 Service listings
Services must state scope, coverage area, pricing basis (hourly or fixed) and any licences required.

## P12 Tags
Up to 10 relevant tags. No competitor brands or unrelated trending words.
